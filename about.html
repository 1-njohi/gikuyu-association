About Us

The House of Mumbi — DeKUT's cultural heartbeat.

We, the members of The DeKUT Gikuyu Students Association, students of Dedan Kimathi University of Technology, guided by the values of unity, identity, integrity, and mutual responsibility, do hereby establish this Association.

Inspired by the spirit of Mumbi, the mother of the Kikuyu people, we come together to preserve our shared heritage, promote moral uprightness, foster leadership, and support the academic, social, and economic well-being of our members.
